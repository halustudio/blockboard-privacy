BLOCK BOARD: RIVALS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Block Board: Rivals, a mobile puzzle game developed and published by HALU Studio.

HALU Studio respects your privacy and is committed to providing transparency about how information is handled when you use our game.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

2. Information We Collect

Block Board: Rivals does not currently require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, or phone number.

Game progress, including scores and saved gameplay information, is stored locally on your device.

HALU Studio does not currently operate a backend server that receives or stores your gameplay progress.

However, third-party advertising services integrated into the game may automatically collect certain information, as described below.

3. Advertising

Block Board: Rivals uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google.

The game may display the following types of advertisements:

  • Banner advertisements
  • Interstitial advertisements
  • Rewarded advertisements

Google AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Device information and operating system details
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Advertising identifiers, where available
  • Advertisement impressions and interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information

This information may be used for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and other purposes described in Google's privacy documentation.

Advertising data collection may occur even when test advertisements are displayed.

For more information, please visit:

Google Privacy Policy

Google AdMob Privacy Information

4. How Information Is Used

Information processed through advertising services may be used to:

  • Display advertisements within the game
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Prevent advertising fraud and abuse
  • Maintain advertising service reliability

Local gameplay information is used to maintain your scores, saved progress, and gameplay experience.

5. Data Storage and Security

Block Board: Rivals currently stores gameplay progress locally on your device.

HALU Studio does not currently provide cloud-based account synchronization or online account recovery.

Removing the application or clearing its storage may permanently delete locally stored game progress.

Device operating systems may provide their own backup and restoration features.

Information processed by Google AdMob is handled according to Google's applicable privacy and security practices.

6. Children's Privacy

Block Board: Rivals is intended for players aged 13 and older.

The game is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

HALU Studio does not knowingly request personal information directly from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the email address below.

We will review the request and take appropriate action where necessary.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your device and location, you may be able to manage advertising preferences through your device settings or Google's privacy controls.

Where required by applicable law, advertising consent choices will be provided through the game or relevant advertising services.

You may remove locally stored gameplay information by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the game, subject to device backup settings.

If you have questions about your privacy rights or information processed through the game, please contact HALU Studio.

8. Third-Party Services

The current version of Block Board: Rivals uses Google AdMob for advertising.

The game does not currently use third-party account authentication, cloud gameplay storage, or online multiplayer services.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies governing how they process information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

HALU Studio may update this Privacy Policy when game features, advertising services, data practices, or legal requirements change.

Any updates will be published on this page, together with a revised "Last updated" date.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
